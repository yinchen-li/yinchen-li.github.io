About

Yinchen Li (李胤辰) is an undergraduate student at Beijing Institute of Technology, majoring in Computer Science and Technology.

His primary interests lie in artificial intelligence, computer vision, multimodal learning, and embodied AI, with a particular focus on Vision-and-Language Navigation (VLN). He is especially interested in how intelligent agents perceive and understand visual environments, follow natural-language instructions, and make effective decisions in complex scenarios.

About Me

My academic interests span both algorithmic research and practical software development. I have experience with C/C++, Java, Kotlin, Python, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and I enjoy applying these technologies in research-oriented and engineering projects.

My current study and research involve reading academic papers, reproducing existing methods, conducting experiments, and exploring new ideas related to VLN, multimodal learning, and embodied intelligence. I also value hands-on development as an important way to deepen my understanding of computer science and intelligent systems.

This website serves as a personal space for documenting my technical notes, paper reviews, research progress, project experiences, and reflections accumulated throughout my studies.
